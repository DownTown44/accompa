accompa — four design directions

Same layout in every direction: slim mode strip (Listen, Explore), Piano | Guitar toggle, '?' shortcuts hint, live chord readout, instrument as hero. Each direction shows Explore with piano (hero), Explore with guitar (voicing stepper), and the Listen state with one wrong guess disabled, in light and dark. Chord roles by shape and color: root = ringed filled circle, 3rd = diamond, 5th = square, 7th = triangle. Frames are 1280×800 shown at 50%.

Direction 1Folio

Folio is the airy, editorial option: wide margins, hairline rules instead of boxes, and a very large light italic chord name that reads like a chapter heading. Chrome is just words and underlines, so the instrument is the only object on the page. Later I would pick Cormorant Garamond Italic (or EB Garamond Italic); the system fallback here is Iowan Old Style / Palatino / Georgia.

Direction 2Bench

Bench is the dense, tool-like option: a slim 56px strip, a left-aligned chord readout at modest size, tight 4px radii, chips and key-hint labels on every control. It trades romance for information and speed, with italic kept for the readout, modes and labels. Later I would pick IBM Plex Sans Italic (or Source Sans 3 Italic); the system fallback here is Segoe UI Variable.

Direction 3Pebble

Pebble is the soft, rounded option: pill-shaped modes, toggle and buttons filled with pastel tints, large radii on keys and interval buttons, and no hard borders at all. Italic is bolder and friendlier, so the app feels approachable and relaxed. Later I would pick Fraunces (soft, italic) or Newsreader Italic; the system fallback here is Palatino.

Direction 4Plate

Plate is the graphic, high-contrast option: thick ink outlines, solid pastel blocks, a huge bold italic chord name, and a reversed (ink-filled) active mode. Roles are filled in full pastel and outlined heavily, so shape and color read at a glance. Later I would pick DM Serif Display Italic or Playfair Display Black Italic; the system fallback here is bold Georgia italic.
