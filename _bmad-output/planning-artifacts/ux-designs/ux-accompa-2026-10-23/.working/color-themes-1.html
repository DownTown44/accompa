Pebble · five color themes

Same Pebble layout, radii, pills, italic type and role shapes (root = ringed + filled circle, 3rd = diamond, 5th = square, 7th = triangle) in every theme. Themes differ in emotional register. One shared selected-state treatment (accent-filled pill, dark text, semibold) is used by both the Listen|Explore strip and the Piano|Guitar toggle. The selected accent is never green; role colors here avoid green too (coral/rose, butter/sand, blue/violet, mauve/lilac). Muted text was nudged until it reaches 4.5:1 on bg, surface and interval tints; pastels are fills only, with dark text on them.
