## Findings

### Behavior and boundaries between stories

1. **The only real defect of the epic came from the browser check, not the reviewers.** In Story 2.1 the parent's headless Chrome run showed Listen lighting the Explore notes after `M` (`useLitNotes.ts` fell back to the workspace). The three reviewers had read that diff and did not report it (`spec-2-1-...md` triage log). Fixed in `f721c02`. Disposition: **accept** (fixed). Prevention: keep the browser check in the parent's step (already a lesson from Epic 1).
2. **The instrument jumped when a note was added (user-found after merge).** Apor reported it on the Story 2.1 PR; the prompt was 14px of text and the readout is 84px. Fixed in `776c399`. The Story 2.1 spec matrix had no row for layout stability; the 2.2 spec added one and the browser check measured it (instrument top stayed at 216px). Disposition: **accept** (fixed). Prevention: that matrix row, now in 2.2 and 2.3.
3. **Switching mode still moves the instrument by 16px** (medium). The epic-wide smoke on `main` measured the instrument's top edge at 216px in Explore and 232px in Listen (`.stage svg`, headless Chrome 1280 x 800). The Listen readout block and the Explore one are different heights. Same complaint as finding 2, one screen over. Disposition: **fix now** (Story 2.4).

### Duplication and pattern divergence
