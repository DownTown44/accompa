### User findings (Apor, in the retro session)

Source for each is Apor's own report on 2026-10-24; they are requested changes, not defects against the specs. Each is routed to Story 1.13 in `epics.md`.

17. **Help hint `?` should not be italic.** The design spec makes it italic (`DESIGN.md`, Help hint); the user wants it upright. Disposition: **fix now** (Story 1.13). Changes the design.
18. **A correct guess should reveal, not jump to the next interval.** Story 1.7's criterion says the next interval auto-plays after a correct guess (`epics.md`, Story 1.7). Apor wants the answer shown, then `Enter` for the next. Disposition: **fix now** (Story 1.13); supersedes part of 1.7.
19. **The large focus border on the piano and fretboard after a click is annoying.** It comes from Story 1.11, which moves focus to the instrument after a pointer click (`src/ui/listen/focus.ts`). Disposition: **fix now** (Story 1.13); keep the ring for keyboard focus.
20. **Changing Direction or switching Root to Fixed applies only from the next question.** Apor has to click Fixed, Reveal, Next before he can use it. The specs said "applies from the next question" (Story 1.9 and 1.12 criteria). He wants the round to restart. Decisions he made: a new question is drawn silently and `Space` plays it; for Fixed with no root, the question is dropped and his next note click sets the root and draws a new question on it. Disposition: **fix now** (Story 1.13); supersedes parts of 1.9 and 1.12.
21. **Clicking outside the Setup panel should close it, with a backdrop.** Not in any spec; the panel was specified as non-modal. Disposition: **fix now** (Story 1.13). Assumption recorded: the backdrop is transparent and swallows the click.

Prevention for 17 to 21: the specs were met, and these are changes found only in use. The retro lesson is that a spec matrix needs rows for the pictured use (hover then click, switch a setting and immediately try it), which is action item 7.
