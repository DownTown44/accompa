What got decided

ModesListen and Explore, plus an always-visible Piano / Guitar toggle. The PRD uses the same names.
GuessingOptional guess-by-click in Listen. Correct advances, wrong stays marked, Reveal is free. No score.
Chord buildingClick notes. The name shows live, alternatives are clickable and change the root. Root-and-quality picking is a Phase 2 chord library.
VoicingsGuitar only in Phase 1. Span 4 frets, open strings free, root and 3rd always present, 7th mandatory on seventh chords, only the 5th droppable.
Inversions, samplesPhase 2.
Persisted settingsSound choice, theme, interval pool. Nothing else.
LookPebble direction, Rose Quartz theme, sage-mint 5th, Palatino stack (swappable later).
SetupGear icon beside the ? hint opens the panel. Listen layout accepted.
KeysTab stays native. M switches mode. I swaps instrument. Shortcuts yield to focused controls.
Accepted limitsPointer-only note toggling and fret-region drag, laptop screens only, Listen needs hearing, screen readers best-effort.
